Privacy Policy for Calculator Hide Photo Video App

Effective Date: 07-10-2026

In short: the photos, videos, files, notes and contacts you hide stay on your phone. We never see them. Only if you choose to back up to Google Drive are they copied, encrypted, to your own Google Drive, where we have no access. The app shows ads and uses Google services for ads, purchases, crash reports and basic usage statistics; these never include your hidden content.

Introduction

Thank you for choosing Calculator Hide Photo Video ("the app", "we", "us"). Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles, where it is kept, and which third-party services are involved.

Your hidden content

Photos, videos, files, notes, contacts and camera captures that you hide are moved into a hidden folder on your phone's storage, which the app opens only after you enter your PIN (or use your fingerprint, if you turned that on). We do not upload, access or process this content on our servers, and we do not share it with anyone.

  • The hidden folder stays on your phone even if the app is uninstalled, so a reinstall can find your files again. To remove your files, unhide or delete them inside the app.
  • Your PIN, fake PIN and security answer are stored on your phone only as one-way scrambled values (hashes), never in readable form.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock is handled entirely by Android; the app never receives your biometric data.

Intruder Selfie

If you turn on Intruder Selfie, the app uses the front camera to take a photo when someone enters a wrong PIN. These photos are saved only in the app on your phone and are never uploaded.

Private browser

The built-in browser connects to the websites you visit, like any browser. It does not keep history, cookies or cache after you close it. Files you download with it are saved into your vault. We do not see the sites you visit.

Backups

Backup to a file

You can save an encrypted backup file to a location you choose. The file is protected with the password you set; we never see the file or the password.

Backup to Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to back up to Google Drive, you sign in with your Google account and allow the app to use its own private app folder in your Google Drive. This permission (drive.appdata) only lets the app see and manage the files it created itself; it cannot see any of your other Drive files.

  • Your backup is encrypted on your phone before it is uploaded. It is stored in your own Google Drive and uses your Drive storage.
  • The backup can only be opened with your vault PIN or your security answer. Someone who gets into your Google account still cannot open it without one of them.
  • We do not operate any server for backups and have no access to your backups, your PIN or your Google account.
  • If you turn on automatic backup, the app uploads a new backup about once a week when your phone is on Wi-Fi. The two newest backups are kept and older ones are deleted.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party apps and services). Backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them, for example from Google Drive's settings under "Manage apps".

Google's handling of your Google account and Drive data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services. They may collect information about your device and how the app is used, as described below. None of them receive your hidden content, PIN or backups.

  • Google AdMob (ads): shows ads in the free version. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information to show and measure ads, and may show personalized ads depending on your settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). See how Google uses information from apps that use its ad services.
  • Firebase Analytics: collects basic, anonymous usage statistics such as that the vault was opened, that onboarding was finished or that an ad was shown, along with device and app information and an app instance ID. It never includes file names, file contents, notes or your PIN.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: when the app crashes, sends a crash report (technical details of the error, device model and Android version) so we can fix problems.
  • Firebase Remote Config: downloads app settings from Firebase, using an installation ID.
  • Google Play Billing: handles Premium purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never receive your payment details.
  • Google Play In-App Review: may show Google Play's rating prompt.

More information: Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Permissions

  • Storage / All files access: to move the files you choose into the hidden folder and back.
  • Camera: for the in-app camera and Intruder Selfie. Used only when you use these features.
  • Internet: for ads, the private browser, Google Drive backup, purchases, crash reports and statistics.
  • Foreground service: so a large automatic Drive backup can finish without being stopped; a notification is shown while it runs.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location, and we do not sell any data. We have no account system and no servers that store your personal information.

Security

Your PIN is stored only as a hash, backups are encrypted, and hidden files are kept in a folder only the app opens. However, hidden files on the phone itself are not encrypted, and no method of storage is completely secure. We recommend that you choose a PIN that is hard to guess and keep a backup of your data to avoid accidental loss.

Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed at individuals under the age of 16 ("children"). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children through our application. If we discover that a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete such information from our records.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
